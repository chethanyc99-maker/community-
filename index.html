📡 Connect Mobile Phones & Tablets

Run Prerana across Wi-Fi, Ethernet, LAN, or Mobile Cellular Networks

📶 Option 1: Same Wi-Fi or LAN

Connect Phones & Tablets at Home or School

1. Connect your phone/tablet to the same Wi-Fi or Hotspot.

2. Open Chrome or Safari and enter this URL:

📷 Scan with your phone's camera to open instantly!

🌐 Option 2: Mobile Network (4G/5G / WAN)

Access Outside Wi-Fi (Over the Internet)

If students are on cellular mobile data (4G/5G) or outside the school:

  1. Double-click run_wan_online.bat in your project folder.
  2. It generates a secure public HTTPS link (e.g. https://...loca.lt).
  3. Share that link — it works on ANY smartphone or network anywhere in the world!
💡 100% Offline Capability: Once opened on a phone, tap "📲 Install App" or "Add to Home Screen". The entire app is cached locally and will work even with mobile data completely turned off!
